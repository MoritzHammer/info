2. Hosting, DNS & Server Log Files

This static website is self-hosted on a private Proxmox/Docker infrastructure running Nginx and routed through Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.) for DNS, TLS encryption, and DDoS/bot protection. When you visit this site, technically necessary access data (IP address, timestamp, requested path, HTTP status code, user agent) is processed to ensure reliable and secure operation (Art. 6 (1) lit. f GDPR).

3. Self-Hosted Web Fonts (Zero External CDN Requests)

All web fonts used on this website (Big Shoulders Display, Instrument Sans, and Instrument Serif) are hosted locally as .woff2 files on this server. No connection is made to Google Fonts or any external font CDN.

4. Zero Cookies, Zero Tracking — Local Preferences Only

This site sets no marketing, analytics, or tracking cookies and loads no third-party analytics scripts. To preserve your chosen display preferences, strictly functional browser storage is used pursuant to § 25 (2) No. 2 TDDDG:

  • localStorage (mh_theme, mh_lang): remembers your Day/Night mode and DE/EN language preference locally in your browser.
  • sessionStorage (mh_intro_seen): prevents the opening logo animation from replaying during the same browser session.

5. Contact by Email

If you contact me via email, the information you provide is processed solely to handle your inquiry (Art. 6 (1) lit. b or lit. f GDPR) and is deleted once it is no longer needed and no statutory retention obligations apply.

6. External Links (GitHub & LinkedIn)

References to GitHub and LinkedIn are standard hyperlinks, not embedded social plugins. Personal data is only transmitted to those platforms if you actively click a link.

7. Your Rights Under the GDPR

You have the right to request access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), and to object to processing (Art. 21). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Baden-Württemberg, the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (LfDI Baden-Württemberg).
